Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This policy covers KNC YouTube Uploader, YouTube Uploader and Myth Channels Publisher (“the apps”), private tools run by Khuê Nguyễn on his own computer to publish to his own YouTube channels and Facebook Pages.

Data the apps access

  • YouTube (Google): with the scopes youtube.upload and youtube.force-ssl, the apps upload videos, set titles, descriptions, thumbnails, captions and publish times, and manage playlists and channel settings of the signed-in channel.
  • Facebook (Meta): with Page permissions, the apps publish posts and Reels to Pages I manage and read their basic insights.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to publish and manage my own content. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising or used to train AI models.
  • OAuth tokens are stored only on my own computer, in files readable by my user account only. No server or database receives them.
  • The apps keep only local logs of what was published (video IDs, URLs, times).

Google API Services User Data Policy

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The apps also follow the YouTube Terms of Service; see the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections (Google) or in Facebook Settings → Business integrations. Revoking makes the stored tokens useless; on request I delete the local token files and logs.
